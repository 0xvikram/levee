## Who to talk to

- Hedera / Stablecoin Studio team members at the venue (track sponsors).
- Teams building payouts, remittance or treasury products on Hedera or any chain.
- Security people: auditors, incident responders, people who handled a key-compromise incident.
- DAO treasury signers.

## Questions to ask

Keep it short. Show the 60-second demo first (attack, freeze, wipe, 2-of-3 reopen), then ask.

1. Who holds the keys that can move or mint your tokens today, and on what kind of machine?
2. If one of those keys leaked tonight, how would you find out, and how long would it take to freeze?
3. Would you put a gate in front of every treasury payout if it added about 4 seconds per payout? Where would that hurt?
4. What would a false alarm cost you (a frozen treasury for a few minutes)? How many per month would you accept?
5. Who in your team decides to buy something like this, and what would they need to see first (audit, pilot, reference)?
6. What would you pay per month to protect one treasury? What do you pay today for similar tools (monitoring, custody, multisig)?
7. Can we run a two-week testnet pilot on your existing token? Who should we follow up with?

## After each conversation

- Write the row the same day.
- Note any change to the product or plan it caused, with a link to the commit or issue.
- Send the follow-up you promised and record the reply as a new row (that is a feedback cycle).
