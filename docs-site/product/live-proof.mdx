## Gate refusals seen live

`no-pass` (machine failing the remote-access check, 347 ms), `over-transfer-cap` (669 ms), `unknown-destination` (969 ms). After signed requests: unsigned gives `bad-signature`, the laptop's key claiming `payouts-server` gives `bad-signature`, the laptop as itself gives `wrong-role`, a replay gives `replayed-request`, a stale request gives `stale-request`, and `/enrol` without the token gives 401.
