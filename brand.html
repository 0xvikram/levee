RuleDetail
One accentYellow appears only on primary buttons and on the single italic accent word in a heading. Never on body text, borders or charts.
Landing theme (v3, Nov 2026)Parley look: --cobalt ground, --cream text (--cream-dim secondary), cream-on-cobalt engravings. --yellow #F2E34C only as small accents: the accent words in headings, pills, the primary button (cobalt-ink text), focus rings, the Levee bar. Cobalt glass cards: cream 6–9% fill, 1px cream 18% border, backdrop blur. No other colours (tested in contrast.test.ts). Each engraving sits on its own sampled sky colour and is feathered with a CSS mask, never boxed; one faint grain + halftone overlay covers the whole page.
Gold is retired--gold #7A5A00 is no longer used anywhere. No gold, brown or tan colours. Yellow is used only on dark grounds (cobalt, ink, moss), where it reaches 9–15:1; never yellow text on cream. Text must always reach 4.5:1 (tested in dashboard/src/lib/__tests__/contrast.test.ts).
Three colours per frameAny screen or slide uses the image plus at most ink, one ground colour and yellow.
Status text on dark groundsOn ink or moss, status text uses the lit tints: --olive-lit #B6C383 for success and contained (9.2:1 on ink), --amber #D9B25C for refusals and warnings (8.7:1), --breach-lit #F08C74 for a live breach (7.2:1). Plain --olive and --breach stay for fills, borders and frames only, since as text on ink they reach just 3.0:1 and 3.6:1. Refusals are amber, never red.
Red is a state, not a colour--breach is used only in the dashboard while a breach is live, then fades back to olive when contained.
Dark sectionsLanding: cobalt grounds with --cream text. Dashboard (/live): --ink/--moss with --sky text. Yellow stays the accent.

04 · Imagery and motion

Calm after the storm

Do

Landing (v3, Nov 2026): cream-on-cobalt engravings: the levee coast with floating islands for the hero, no imagery in the sections between (colour, glass, rules and halftone only; the product window is a cobalt mock until the demo video returns), the levee coastline along the footer (links over its empty sky). Pixel-dither reveal, subtle shimmer, words rise in on scroll, all motion off under reduced motion. The dusk video and meadow photography are retired from the landing.

Don't

Glowing blockchain cubes, hacker hoodies, padlocks, red alarms on the site, neon gradients, stock office photos, fast cuts, camera shake, text inside images. Never remove AI-content watermarks; frame them instead.
