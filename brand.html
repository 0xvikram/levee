RuleDetail
One accentYellow appears only on primary buttons and on the single italic accent word in a heading. Never on body text, borders or charts.
Accent word contrastOn light grounds (sky, haze, bright parts of the hero) the accent word uses --gold #7A5A00 (5.1:1). Bright --yellow is used for the accent word only on dark grounds: moss, ink, the dusk footer, the dashboard (9.4–13:1). Text must always reach 4.5:1.
Three colours per frameAny screen or slide uses the image plus at most ink, one ground colour and yellow.
Status text on dark groundsOn ink or moss, status text uses the lit tints: --olive-lit #B6C383 for success and contained (9.2:1 on ink), --amber #D9B25C for refusals and warnings (8.7:1), --breach-lit #F08C74 for a live breach (7.2:1). Plain --olive and --breach stay for fills, borders and frames only, since as text on ink they reach just 3.0:1 and 3.6:1. Refusals are amber, never red.
Red is a state, not a colour--breach is used only in the dashboard while a breach is live, then fades back to olive when contained.
Dark sectionsUse --moss ground with --sky text; yellow stays the accent.

04 · Imagery and motion

Calm after the storm

Do

Surreal cinematic photography: wide meadow of small white flowers, a lone figure from behind, huge floating earth islands with hanging roots, warm god rays through haze, film grain, shallow depth of field. Empty sky at centre-top for the headline. Slow 8-second loops: drifting haze, swaying grass, light shafts moving. Words rise in on scroll.

Don't

Glowing blockchain cubes, hacker hoodies, padlocks, red alarms on the site, neon gradients, stock office photos, fast cuts, camera shake, text inside images. Never remove AI-content watermarks; frame them instead.
